Japanese frontend: r9y9/open_jtalk, commit 462fc38e7520aa89e4d32b2611749208528c901e (BSD).
Dictionary: open_jtalk_dic_utf_8-1.11.tar.gz (license in OpenJTalk-dictionary.txt).
Label pipeline adapted from pyopenjtalk 0.4.1 (MIT).
Phoneme/prosody logic adapted from High-Logic/Genie-TTS (MIT).

English frontend: Genie-TTS CMU/name dictionaries, hot dictionary, NLTK averaged
perceptron POS model, and g2p_en checkpoint20 GRU, from High-Logic/Genie resources
revision 52b17272e0b7032415e85ad37b551db2386b1810.
The GRU weights are exported to ONNX without quantization; phoneme vocabulary
and recurrent computation follow Genie-TTS CleanG2p. POS inference is ported
from NLTK; normalization uses an Android implementation for ordinary prose.
CMU dictionary (BSD), NLTK and g2p_en (Apache-2.0) licenses are included here.
Resource hashes and provenance are in language_frontends/*/manifest.json.
